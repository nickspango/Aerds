Aerds, biography

Music as an escape, passion as devotion.

It all begins at the age of nine, in front of the music video for Robot Rock. Even then, he knows that music will become his calling. He starts composing at 14 on GarageBand Mobile, then moves to FL Studio at 16. A year later, he releases his first single, Deadline, which reaches 1.2 million views on YouTube and ranks seventh in Synthspiria magazine’s Singles of the Year list, a leading publication in the synthwave scene at the time.

Despite this first success, his father destroys his computer to discourage him from pursuing music. From that moment on, a vital need to create and to rise above is born.

He leaves his law studies, buys his first synthesizers and spends several years building a complete and coherent artistic project, refusing to release music simply for the sake of putting it out.

His return comes in 2024, almost by accident, following a TikTok video. Asomnium, a track with ambient influences, gains momentum across social media, with several trends using the track accumulating hundreds of millions of views and close to 1.5 million streams. He follows this with Renascienta (March 2025), which notably earns him a support slot for Aaron Hibell at Le Trabendo in Paris, as well as a sync placement in a Chivas campaign featuring Charles Leclerc.

In March 2026, he contributes to Bleached, a compilation by the Rêves collective, alongside Realo, Idée Noire and Reckless on the track J’ressens plus rien. The compilation also features Era, a more personal track composed in 2018. These releases are followed by a performance in Paris in May 2026, as part of the Rêves Tour.

With Hirā (July 2026), Aerds continues to develop his project around a hybrid electronic music, now fully embraced, turned toward club culture, live performance and visual storytelling. His latest single, Alpes (November 2026), marks a new chapter, leading up to his first EP of the same name, scheduled for January 2027.
